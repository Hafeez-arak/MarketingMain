# Dashboard, Assistant and Settings

The pages you use every day around the main cycle.

## Getting in

1. Go to the app and press **Request access**. Enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters), then confirm the email link.
2. You wait on an approval screen until an admin approves you. Press **Check again** after they do; the page does not refresh by itself.
3. Once approved you see the companies the admin gave you.

## Dashboard

The home page. Top buttons: **Plan a month**, **View analytics**, **View calendar**, **Create post**.

- **Numbers**: followers, reach, views and engagement across the connected accounts. Tick platforms to narrow it, pick a date range, and press **Refresh** for fresh figures.
- **Website**: how the site does on Google, with recommended fixes.
- **Needs attention**: posts made but not booked, or failed. **Open the post queue** to fix them.
- **Research**: the headline of the latest research run, dated. If a run stopped early, it says why. **Read the full research** opens the Research page.

## Assistant

Ask anything about this company's data in plain words, for example "Which posts did best last month?" or "What did competitors post this week?".

- Open it from **Assistant** in the sidebar, or from any page with ⌘K.
- It answers from our own data and says when a sample is too small to mean anything.
- It can propose rules, ideas and drafts. It cannot publish or schedule anything.
- **New conversation** starts fresh; earlier chats are kept.
- The Assistant page also shows the **Weekly research** runs and a button to run research now (5–10 minutes, paid from the monthly budget).

## Notifications

The bell at the top right shows app messages, such as "Brand Brain profile saved". **Clear all** empties it.

## Settings (companies)

Each company has its own Brand Brain, plans, posts and numbers, seen only by the people given access to it.

- **Add company**: name it and tick who should have access.
- **Rename** or **Switch** to a company. You can also switch from the company name at the top of the sidebar.
- **Delete company** (Danger Zone) permanently removes everything in it. You need at least two companies to delete one.

## Team & Access

Admins decide who can sign in and which companies each person sees. The sidebar shows a count when requests are waiting.

- **Access requests**: everyone who signs up lands here first and sees nothing until approved. **Approve** or **Deny**.
- **Add someone** clears an email in advance, before they sign up. Nothing is emailed; tell them to sign up.
- **People with access**: tick or untick companies per person; changes apply immediately. **Remove** takes all access away; nothing they made is deleted, and restoring is one click.
- Access levels: **Marketing app** (everything in their companies), **Sales** (the leads list only) and **Sales admin** (leads plus sending to Odoo).

Only the administrator can add, remove or change people. Everyone else should ask them.
