# Content Planning

A monthly plan takes you from "what should we post?" to booked posts in four steps: **Ideas → Pictures → Captions → Save**. Saving books every post on Instagram and LinkedIn at its date and time (KSA). Open it under **Content Planning** in the sidebar.

## The plans list

- Every monthly plan, with its approved ideas. Click one to open it where you left off.
- **New monthly plan** (or **Plan a month** on the Dashboard) starts a new one.
- **Delete** removes a plan. Any posts it already booked are cancelled first; ones that have gone out stay published.

## Step 1: Ideas

1. **Pick the month.** The app lists Saudi moments that fall in it (Ramadan, Eids, Founding Day, National Day) if you tick that box. Optionally pick which days of the week you post.
2. **Add your posts.** Type each post you already know you want, for example "Announce the new Riyadh showroom opening", and pick its platform and format. The AI turns each short line into a full brief.
3. **Let the AI suggest more** (optional). Tick it, then set roughly how many, a focus for the month, focus categories (Product launch, Project showcase, Education…), and a target mix such as "40% product, 20% educational". The AI uses the Brand Brain, the latest research, past posts and approved rules.
4. Press **Create plan with AI ideas**. Writing the month takes a few minutes and runs on the server: you can close the tab. **Do not start a second plan for the same month while one is running**; both finish and both are charged.

Then review the ideas, in a **List** or **Calendar** view, filtered by All, Undecided, Approved or Rejected:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Approve / Reject | Decide one idea. Add a reason when rejecting; it teaches the AI |
| Approve all / Reject all / Reset | Decide every idea at once |
| Edit | Change the wording yourself |
| Change with AI | Say what to change ("less salesy, about the showroom") and the AI rewrites it |
| Delete | Remove the idea |
| Suggest more with AI | Adds more ideas without repeating the existing ones; useful after rejecting some |

Ideas marked **Built from an idea the research agent proposed** came from the Research page. Mix and Target show how the plan compares with the mix you asked for.

## Step 2: Pictures

Press **Next — pictures**. Every approved idea needs its media:

- Pick a photo from the Brand Brain asset library, upload your own, or press to make one in the Creative Studio. A Studio picture comes straight back to this post.
- **Edit/Add images** changes the platform, format, slides and shape. Carousels can hold 2–10 slides; drag or use ‹ › to reorder.
- The fitter lets you **crop to the edges** or **show all, add a background**. "Put every other slide in this shape too" matters on Instagram, which crops a carousel to the first slide's shape.
- Text posts and polls skip this step.

## Step 3: Captions

Press **Next — captions**. The AI writes three captions from each picture and its idea.

- **Pick one caption**, then edit it if you like. **Choose another** reopens the three; **Write 3 captions** asks for a fresh set; **Write my own** lets you type it.
- Check the **date and time** (KSA). An empty date is filled automatically when you save; a past date must be changed or the post won't be scheduled.
- LinkedIn polls: write the question and answers and choose how long it runs.

## Step 4: Save

Press save. Each post is booked for its date and time on its platform. Anything that can't be booked waits in the **Post Queue** under Needs attention. There is no second approval. Posts that have already gone out are locked and show **View post ↗**.

## Formats you can plan

| Platform | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | Feed image, Carousel (2–10 slides), Reel (video), Story |
| LinkedIn | Text post, Single image, Multi-image, Video, Poll |

LinkedIn posts now publish to the company page, the same as Instagram.
