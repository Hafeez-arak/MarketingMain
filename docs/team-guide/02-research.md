# Research

The research agent reads the market every Monday at 06:00 (KSA) and writes a report on what changed since last week. Open it under **Research** in the sidebar. The page has two halves: **Market research** (what is happening outside) and **What we learned** (what our own posts and decisions taught us).

## Running it

- It runs on its own every Monday. To run it now, press **Run research**. It takes about 5–10 minutes; you can leave the page while it works.
- Each run is paid from the company's monthly agent budget. If the budget runs out, the report says so instead of pretending it was a quiet week.
- The cards at the top show: rules in use, suggestions waiting for review, dated actions, and how many of our posts were measured. **Holding it back** lists anything missing in setup (for example no connected account), with a link to fix it.

## The report, section by section

It opens on a short **at a glance** card, then tabs with counts. Each item is a two-line title, why it matters, and what to do. Use the **For** filter to show only Business items or everything.

| Section | What it tells you | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top priorities | What most needs doing this week, most important first | Act on them |
| Content ideas | Gaps between what the market wants and what we say, with post ideas | Send ideas to a content plan |
| Website and search | How the website did on Google over the last 28 days | Open Analytics for every number |
| How our channels did | Our own Instagram and LinkedIn numbers | Read only |
| Leads, tenders and projects | Every open opening we track, not only this week's | Set a status: new, assigned, pursued, won, lost, dropped |
| Competitor moves | What each competitor did, and what it means for us | Read only |
| New competitors to review | Companies acting like competitors that we don't watch yet | **Accept** to watch, **Reject** so it is never suggested again |
| What competitors are posting | Their social activity | Read only |
| Events and expos | Shows where our buyers gather, next 90 days and later this year | Mark our plan: visiting, exhibiting, sponsoring, skipping |
| Dates to plan content around | Dated moments worth a post | Use when planning |
| Market and technical news | Regulations, standards, technology, giga-projects | Read; some items have a note for the technical team |
| Global industry | Manufacturers, prices and standards abroad, which reach us later | Read only |
| Smart poles | Our municipal line, kept separate because its buyer is a city | Read only |
| How this research went | Topics checked, what did not complete, unanswered questions, all sources | Check if something looks missing |

**What this adds up to** writes a three-sentence summary of the whole report on request.

## What the research watches

At the bottom, open **What the research watches** to steer next week's run:

- **Competitors it watches**: add one by name, add their Instagram handle, or press **Find handles**. A competitor is only measured once its handle is confirmed. Suggestions from the agent wait for you to accept or reject.
- **Standing questions**: questions asked every run so weeks stay comparable, for example "Is anyone pushing tunnel lighting?". Accept, pause or delete them.

This list belongs to the research and can differ from the competitors in the Brand Brain.

## What we learned

The second half of the page. It fills from two places: the ideas you approve or reject in content plans, and the numbers from published posts.

- **What happened**: approval rate, why ideas were turned down, what people edit most, which ideas needed the most re-drafts.
- **What performed**: results by content pillar, format, weekday and platform. Until enough posts have gone out it says the sample is too small.
- **Suggested rules**: press **Run review** and the AI proposes rules from the history above. **Approve** (or edit, then **Save & approve**) and the rule starts shaping every plan and caption. Approving never changes what the Brand Brain says.
- **Rules in use**: every active rule. **Retire** stops one; **Delete** removes it.

If a suggested rule mentions a date, the page warns you: rules never expire, so a one-off belongs in a content plan instead.

## Other views

- **Download PDF** opens a printable version of the report to share or save.
- **Business view** shows where we stand against competitors per business line (lighting, controls): who we compete with, who beats us in bids, what changed, and what we still can't answer. Bid results come from **Record a bid**, filled in by our own team.
