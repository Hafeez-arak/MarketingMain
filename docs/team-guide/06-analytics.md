# Analytics

Analytics shows real numbers pulled from the connected accounts and the website. A number the app could not measure shows as "—", never as 0.

## Using the page

- At the top, pick what to view: **Instagram**, **LinkedIn**, or **Website**.
- Pick a date range (or a custom one).
- **Refresh** asks each platform for fresh numbers. Reach and impressions can still lag the platform by up to 48 hours.
- **Performance report** opens the printable report (below).

## Social numbers

| Part of the page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Straight from Instagram | Accounts reached, accounts engaged, profile link taps for the whole account (30 days at most) |
| Your posts, added up | Engagement rate, total followers, posts this period, best post |
| Engagement over time | Likes, comments, shares, saves, views, impressions, reach, clicks per day |
| Top performing posts | Each post with its numbers; **View ↗** opens it on the platform |
| Posts per platform / Posts over time | How much we posted |
| Best time to post | When our posts do best |
| Follower history | Followers per day |
| Posting frequency vs engagement | Whether posting more helps or hurts |
| Engagement accumulation | How fast a post collects its engagement after publishing |

Good to know:

- Followers are counted once a day. A newly connected account has no count until the first one, and its posts can take about 40 minutes to appear.
- Instagram gives at most 29–30 days of account numbers; LinkedIn at most 88 days. LinkedIn has no view counts.
- Posts made directly on LinkedIn or Instagram, outside the app, are counted too.

## Website

Two Google sources on one tab:

- **Website search (Google Search Console)**: how we appear on Google before the click. Impressions, clicks, click rate, average position, brand vs non-brand searches, the search queries people typed, top pages, countries, devices, and indexing problems. **What to do about it** lists actions worked out from these numbers.
- **Website visits (Google Analytics 4)**: what happens after the click. Sessions, visitors, engaged visits, average visit length, where visits came from, which pages they read, key events (form sent, phone tap, catalogue download) and visits arriving from Instagram's bio link.

If a source says "Not connected yet", ask the admin.

## Performance report

A printable summary for a period (last 7, 30 or 88 days): every channel side by side, and the best posts of the period ranked by interactions (likes, comments, shares and saves). Use the browser's print dialog to save it as a PDF and send it on.
