# Publishing and Social

Saved plans publish themselves. These pages are where you check what is going out, fix what failed, move dates, and make one-off posts. Instagram and LinkedIn are live; TikTok stays hidden until an account is connected.

## How a post goes out

1. A post is booked when you save a plan, or when you schedule it from the composer or the Studio.
2. The booking is sent to the platform. The post then goes out on its own at its time, even if nobody has the app open.
3. The app checks the platforms every hour, marks the post Published and shows its link.
4. If booking or publishing fails, the post moves to **Needs attention** in the Post Queue, and the sidebar shows a count.

## Post Queue

Every post from plans and the composer, in four tabs:

| Tab | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Needs attention | Failed posts, posts with no time, and posts whose time has passed |
| Upcoming | Booked posts, soonest first |
| Published | Posts that went out, with a link |
| All | Everything |

On each post you can **Schedule**, **Reschedule**, **Cancel schedule** (the post stays under Needs attention), edit the caption, **Finish in composer**, or **Delete**. Posts that have gone out can't be edited or deleted.

## Schedule (Content Calendar)

- A **month** or **week** view of booked posts, coloured by platform. Switch with Previous / Next.
- **Drag a post** to another day or time to move it. Moving cancels the old slot and books the new one.
- Click a post to see it, change its date and time, **Edit post**, or **Cancel schedule**.
- The strip at the top holds approved posts with no time yet; open one to schedule it.
- Posts published since you last looked show in solid green. **Mark all seen** clears that.
- A warning shows when two posts to the same platform are within an hour of each other.
- **New post** opens the composer.

## Social Media pages

**Social Media** lists each platform: Connected, Needs reconnecting or Not connected, with **Connect** or **Open**.

- **Connecting**: Instagram must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page, one per company. LinkedIn: choose the company page (you must be an admin of it). Connecting asks you to sign in on the platform.
- **Instagram page**: a **Posts** tab (filter Total, Pending, Scheduled, Published) and an **Analytics** tab. Open any post to edit its caption and hashtags, schedule, reschedule, **Approve & publish**, save its image to the Media Library, or delete it.
- **LinkedIn page**: posts made in this app, with totals. Posts made directly on LinkedIn are counted on the Analytics page.

## The composer (Create post)

Open it with **Create post** on the Dashboard, a platform page or the calendar.

1. Choose the accounts under **Publish to**, and optionally a campaign.
2. Add pictures or video (reorder, crop or remove each).
3. Caption: **Enhance with AI** (three captions written from the picture) or write it yourself. Hashtags count toward the caption limit.
4. Platform extras. Instagram: collaborators, tag people, Reel cover frame, audio track, first comment, alt text, "Made with AI" label (on by default). LinkedIn: poll question, answers and duration, link preview, first comment, alt text.
5. Optional: **Analyse this post** gives feedback on what to change before it goes out.
6. Finish with **Save as draft**, **Schedule for later** or **Post now**.

LinkedIn polls can't be edited after they are published.

## Auto-replies

When someone comments a chosen word on Instagram, they get a private message automatically.

1. **New auto-reply**: give it a name (only the team sees it).
2. **Trigger words**, separated by commas, for example `catalogue, price, كتالوج`. Choose to match anywhere or only as a whole word, and whether to catch close misspellings.
3. **Never reply to comments containing** (optional), for example `job, complaint`. Checked first.
4. **The message they receive** (private), and an optional **public reply** under their comment.
5. Choose **every post** or **one specific post**. This can't be changed later.
6. Tick **Also answer people who send the word as a message** to cover direct messages.

Each auto-reply shows Triggered, Sent, Failed and People counts, and **History** lists every reply. You can pause, edit or delete it.
