# Brand Brain

The Brand Brain is the one place the AI learns about the company. Every plan, caption, picture, video, email draft and research run reads it first, so a gap here shows up everywhere. Each company has its own.

## What it holds (Arak Lighting example)

The section list is different for each company. Arak's looks like this:

| Section | What goes in |
| --- | --- |
| Identity & Voice | Who we are, what we sell, how we sound |
| Guardrails | Words, claims and topics the AI must never use |
| Audience | Who we are talking to (architects, consultants, contractors) |
| Market & References | The market we work in and brands we admire |
| Visual | Colours, look and feel for pictures |
| Knowledge Centre | Facts the AI can quote: certifications, projects, specs |
| Asset Library | Logos, product photos, project photos, references, music |
| Products | One row per product line |
| Message Templates | Ready-made replies and messages |
| Suppliers | The brands we carry (keep in step with the website) |

Fields sections are text boxes. Directory sections (Products, Suppliers) are lists with a row per item and **+ Add** to add one.

## Filling it in

1. Type into any box. The dot beside each label turns solid when it has text; the count on each section (for example 4/6) shows how many are filled.
2. Long text: click the expand icon to edit full screen.
3. Press **Save** at the bottom of the page. "Unsaved changes" shows until you do. Directory rows save on their own.
4. A box marked **NOT SENT TO AI** is a team note only; the AI never sees it (prices are kept this way).

At the bottom, **Preview the brand instructions** shows exactly the text the AI receives.

## Asset Library

Upload files by dropping them in or clicking upload. First choose what they are: Product Photo, Project Photo, Logo, Reference, Music or Other.

- Give each file a title, a caption and tags. The caption tells the AI what is in the photo.
- **Project photos** can be grouped by project (for example "Riyadh Air"). Use **Create new project group**, then open a group to add photos or flip through them.
- The logo you upload here is the one the Studio editor offers under "Your logo".
- These are the photos offered when you give a planned post a picture.

## Rules (Learned guidance)

Short one-sentence rules added to every matching generation, on top of the fields. Example: "Do not open captions with a rhetorical question."

- Write your own under **Add a rule**, and choose which tasks it applies to.
- Rules the system suggests are approved under Research → What we learned. They are listed here so the whole rule book reads in one place.
- Retire a rule to stop it without deleting it.

## Changing the structure

Press **Customise structure** to add, rename, hide or delete sections, fields and columns. These changes save at once and affect only this company.

For any field you can set:

- **Send this field to the AI**: off keeps it as a team note.
- **Only send it to**: limit it to some tasks: Idea planning, Caption & copy, Image prompts, Video & motion, Research, Ideation chat. Nothing ticked means every task. Example: a price list helps planning but is wasted in an image prompt.

Deleting a field or section hides it; the text already typed is kept in the database. Press **Done customising** when finished.
