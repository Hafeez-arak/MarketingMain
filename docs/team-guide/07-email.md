# Email Flows

Email has two separate lanes, kept apart so a problem in one can never hurt the other:

| | Newsletters | Outreach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who gets it | People who know us or signed up: customers, partners | Prospects who don't know us yet |
| Looks like | A designed email, or plain text | A short personal plain email, plus follow-ups |
| Sent from | The newsletter sender (for example updates@email.arak-sa.com) | Our outreach mailboxes (for example sales@arak-sa.com) |
| Measured by | Opens, clicks, bounces, spam reports | Replies (no tracking) |

The page has five tabs: **Overview, Contacts, Outreach, Newsletters, Settings**.

## Overview

What is going out today, what came back, and anything that needs you. Warnings appear at the top, for example "No outreach mailbox can send". The rest shows today's numbers for each lane, the last 30 days, and recent campaigns.

Healthy numbers for newsletters: bounces under 2%, spam reports under 0.1%. Open rates run high because Apple Mail opens every email automatically, so trust clicks.

## Contacts

**People** is everyone you can email. Filter by lane (Newsletter / Outreach), group, type and status, or search.

- **Add contact** one at a time. Notes are private; the AI reads them when writing a personal outreach email.
- **Import CSV**: export from Outlook or Excel as CSV, choose the lane, language, type and a group. **Only put people who know Arak in the Newsletter lane.** A bought or scraped list there can get the sending domain blocked in its first week; prospects go in Outreach.
- Tick contacts to act on them together: add to or move between groups, switch lane, set language, mark unsubscribed or active again, or delete.

**Groups** are who campaigns are sent to. **New group** makes one. Automatic groups ("Previously contacted Arak/CLB") are filled by the lead agent from enquiries; you can still add or remove people by hand.

Under Contacts you also see **leads from the research** with public contacts, ready to add to Outreach or dismiss.

## Newsletters

1. **This week's AI drafts**: every Monday morning the AI writes three newsletter ideas from the Brand Brain and the week's research. **Open the draft** to start a campaign from one, **Dismiss** the rest, or **Write this week** for fresh options.
2. **New campaign**: pick the groups and language (Arabic sets right-to-left layout). Write it yourself, press **Write with AI**, or choose a drag-and-drop design template; your logo is already in. Plain text is quickest and can switch to a design later.
3. Fill in subject and preview line. Insert fields such as the first name.
4. **Send or schedule…**: send now, or schedule for a morning. Tuesday to Thursday mornings work best. Today's limit is shown before you send.

After sending, each campaign shows recipients, sent, opened, clicked, bounced and spam reports. **Duplicate** copies a campaign; **Delete** removes a draft.

## Outreach

Three sub-tabs: **Campaigns**, **Mailboxes**, **Replied & signed up**.

**Campaigns** are a first email plus follow-ups to a group of prospects.

1. **New campaign**: pick the prospect group and which mailboxes send it (none ticked = all ready ones share the work).
2. Write the first email, or **Write with AI** (it can write one per prospect from the research). **Vary wording** and **Versions** create A/B/C versions to compare.
3. Add follow-ups, each a set number of days after the previous one. Leave the subject empty to reply in the same thread.
4. Add the **Sign-up button** if you want prospects to join the newsletter.
5. **Start sequence…**: now or on a day. Sending runs Sunday–Thursday 9:00–17:00 Riyadh, one email at a time at uneven times.

Rules that protect us: a reply stops all follow-ups to that person; anyone emailed by another outreach campaign in the last 90 days is skipped; a mailbox with bounces above 5% pauses itself. On a running campaign you can **Pause**, **Edit wording**, **Resume sending** or **Cancel the rest**. Wait for about 100 sends per version before picking a winner.

**Mailboxes**: connect each outreach mailbox (Microsoft 365 or Google), with a real person's name and signature. New mailboxes warm up: 5 emails a day in the first week, 10 in the second, then up to their limit. The **outreach sending switch** at the top turns all outreach on or off; it is also the emergency stop. **What goes out next** previews the next run without sending.

**Replied & signed up**: everyone who replied or pressed Sign-up. A reply moves the person to the Newsletter lane unless they asked to stop.

## Settings

Who newsletters come from (name, address, reply-to inbox), the company address in the footer, and daily and monthly newsletter limits with a six-week warm-up. A health brake can't be switched off: newsletters pause on their own if spam reports reach 0.3% or bounces reach 5% in a week.
