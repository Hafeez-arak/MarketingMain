# Creative Studio and Media Library

The Creative Studio makes pictures and videos from a description, then lets you keep talking to the result until it is right. Everything it makes uses the Brand Brain. The Media Library keeps every file the company has.

## Making something

1. Open **Creative Studio**. Under **What are you making?** pick:
    - **An image**: a post, story or ad visual
    - **A video**: one clip made from a description
    - **A long video**: several shots chained and stitched into one
2. **Describe it**, for example "A dusk shot of a modern Riyadh villa facade with warm linear lighting". Pick the **Shape** (for example 4:5 for an Instagram post).
3. Optional: attach a **reference image** to take inspiration from, or a **style reference**. **Enhance** rewrites your description into a fuller one before anything is made; you see the result first and can undo it.
4. Press generate. For images you get **two options, one from each model** (ChatGPT and Gemini) to compare.

The credit figure at the top shows how much is left for making pictures and videos.

## Improving the result

Each option has its own chat. Type what to change ("make the light warmer, remove the car") and a new version appears under it. Click a picture to see it full size; drag an image from one option into the other to reuse it.

On any version you can:

| Button | What it does | Costs a render? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editor | Add text, logos, shapes; crop and adjust | No |
| Animate | Turn the still into a video clip | Yes |
| Make 1080p | Sharpen a clip to 1080p without changing it | Yes |
| Re-render | Same settings, a fresh take | Yes |
| Download | Save it; it is also filed in the Media Library | No |
| Use this → | Turn it into a post (below) | No |
| Delete | Remove this version from the history | No |

## Video settings

- **Camera move**: presets such as Slow push in, Pull back to reveal, Rise up the facade, Lights come up, Dusk settles, or describe your own. **Strength** sets how strong: Subtle, Medium, Strong.
- **Look**: Architectural film, Golden hour, Night / dusk, Editorial product, Documentary, or none.
- **Model, length and quality**: Draft is for checking the movement; Final is for the post. The cost of each render shows before you press go.
- **Start frame / end frame**: the clip opens or lands on a chosen image.
- **Sound**: optional, and it adds to the cost on some models.

## Long videos (storyboard)

Describe the opening shot, then add shots on the storyboard. Each new shot can **continue from the previous** one or be a **new shot**, joined by a **hard cut** or **crossfade**. A shared **style bible** keeps every clip looking alike. The board shows the finished length and the cost to render everything. When every clip has rendered, press **Stitch into one video**.

## The editor

Free, and it never re-renders the picture or footage.

- **Text**: press T. Fonts, size, colour, shadow, highlight, spacing. Typing Arabic switches the box to right-to-left and an Arabic font on its own.
- **Elements**: shapes, arrows, stars.
- **Your logo**: the logo from the Brand Brain; **Put in corner** places it.
- **Picture**: add an image from the Brand Brain, the Media Library, this session, or upload one.
- **Crop** and **Adjust** (filters, auto-adjust, light, colour). Nothing is baked in; reopen the edit later and every setting is still there.
- **Split into layers**: each object and any painted-in text becomes its own layer you can move or delete (20–40 seconds).
- **Position**: align, layer order, even out gaps. On video, text can animate in and out.

## Use this →: from picture to post

Pick the platforms, write a caption (or press **Draft it for me**), then choose:

- **Send to Post Queue**: waits there for a time
- **Schedule**: pick a date and time
- **Publish now**: goes live immediately

If you came from a content plan, Use this instead attaches the picture to the planned post and sends you back to the plan.

## Chats

The left sidebar keeps every Studio session, grouped by date. **New chat** starts a fresh one; each chat can be renamed or deleted.

## Media Library

Every image, video and document in the company. **Upload** by dropping files in or browsing; **Download** or **Delete** any item. Studio downloads and generated posts land here on their own. Brand photos for the AI (logos, product and project photos) live in the Brand Brain's Asset Library instead.
