# Where ARAK needs to be listed so AI assistants name it

AI assistants answer "who does lighting design in Riyadh?" from what other
sites say, not only from ours. A company that appears on the directories,
brand pages and articles an assistant reads gets named. One that appears only
on its own site mostly does not.

This list was checked on 2026-11-01 by searching the web. After the first
**Named in AI answers** check (Analytics → Website), its "Sources the answers
used" line replaces the guesswork here: those are the exact sites the answers
were built from. Re-rank this list from that line.

Every item below is a listing, an account or a message, so a person does it.
Nothing here is automated.

## What exists today

Searching for ARAK outside arak-sa.com finds job boards (GulfTalent files ARAK
under "Retail"), our own LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram posts, theorg.com,
and **Yandex Maps, which files ARAK as a "management company"**. ARAK is not on
Clutch, Architonic, ArchiExpo or any Saudi business directory we searched.

## The targets, most useful first

1. **Google Business Profile** for the Hittin showroom. See the website repo's
   `docs/ENTITY-SETUP.md`. It feeds Google's map pack and Gemini.
2. **Clutch: "Top Lighting Designers in Saudi Arabia"** (clutch.co/sa/agencies/design/lighting).
   It ranks #3 on Google for "lighting consultant riyadh", our largest non-brand
   search, and lists Alrouf, Av Galaxy, Lighting Experts Middle East and FP Group.
   A free profile is enough to appear. Reviews from two or three real clients
   decide the order.
3. **Partner brands' dealer and distributor pages.** ARAK carries 41 brands, and
   several publish a Saudi dealer list that assistants read as a fact source:
   - Artemide store locator (artemide.com/en/storelocator)
   - Arkoslight sales network (lists only its own Saudi rep today)
   - LEDS C4 dealers (via Architonic)
   - Zennio's and Lutron's partner lists (KNX and controls, where we have named projects)

   Ask each brand that ARAK represents in the Kingdom to list ARAK, with a link to
   arak-sa.com. Each listing is a link and a citation from a site that assistants
   trust.
4. **Architonic and ArchiExpo** dealer profiles. Architects and lighting
   consultants use them, and they rank for brand + country searches.
5. **Correct the wrong listings**: Yandex Maps (category "management company")
   and GulfTalent ("Retail sector"). A wrong category on a directory is repeated
   by every assistant that reads it.
6. **Project credits on other sites.** Riyadh Air, Four Points by Sheraton,
   Solitaire Mall and the Naba AlSaha Hospital award already have LinkedIn posts.
   A short case study on the contractor's or consultant's own site, naming ARAK
   and linking to the project page, is the strongest third-party mention there is.

## How to tell it worked

Run **Named in AI answers** once a month with the same questions. A listing
shows up when the answers that named a competitor start naming ARAK, and when
the site appears among the answers' sources. One check is a sample; the share
over three or four checks is the measure.
